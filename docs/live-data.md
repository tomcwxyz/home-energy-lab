# Live data: first setup

The Home Energy Lab cloud app supports two independent sources.

## Weather
A town-centre location near Newton Aycliffe is configured in server-only Vercel environment variables. This is for approximate weather, not a property-specific location.

- Each sync loads recent temperature and solar-irradiance estimates, and the next three days of hourly forecasts.
- Weather readings are tagged estimated or forecast; they do not count as metered solar generation.
- Forecast power in W/m² is sunlight arriving on a horizontal plane, not kWh generated by your roof.

## Smart meters (Bright)
1. Install Hildebrand Bright on a phone and create an account: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.co.hildebrand.brightionic
2. In the Bright app, verify the smart-meter details and wait for the confirmation. A newly installed gas smart meter may not be available immediately.
3. In Vercel home-energy-lab > Settings > Environment Variables, create encrypted Production secrets:
   - BRIGHT_USERNAME (Bright login)
   - BRIGHT_PASSWORD (Bright password)
4. Redeploy production after creating new environment variables.
5. Sign in to Home Energy Lab and select **Refresh connected data**.
6. The application uses Hildebrand's official /auth, /virtualentity and /resource/{id}/readings endpoints to fetch eight days of 30-minute data. Repeated imports update matching records, not duplicate them.

Do not paste Bright passwords in chat or commit them to GitHub.

## Operation and safety
- Production cron runs at 00:15, 06:15, 12:15, 18:15 UTC each day.
- Manual refresh is available to signed-in dashboard users.
- Each source logs a result, including skipped/needs-setup and failures.
- Existing records are retained if a later sync fails.
- Weather is not actual solar production; local Fronius/diverter sensors are the next integration.
- Bright half-hourly readings may be delayed, unlike real-time monitoring using a local CAD device.
- This dashboard observes and logs only. It never remotely operates the boiler or immersion heater.
